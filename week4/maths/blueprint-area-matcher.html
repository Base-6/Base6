📐 The Blueprint Area Matcher

You are the architect. Area means how much flat space a room covers — and the quick way to find it is length × width. Work through the blueprint, room by room.

Stage 1 of 5

Area is just counting squares

This room is 4 squares across and 3 squares down. You could count all the squares one by one... or you could go faster: 4 × 3.

2. A new room is 5 squares across and 3 down. Without drawing it — what is its area?

Stage 3 of 5

Find the missing side

An architect often knows the area and one side, and has to work out the other. If length × width = area, then area ÷ one side = the other side.

Stage 4 of 5

The L-shaped room

This open-plan room is an L-shape. The trick: split it into two rectangles, find each area, then add them together.

Rectangle A (the top) is 8 m by 5 m.

Area A = m²

Rectangle B (the bottom-left) is 5 m by 4 m.

Area B = m²

Total area of the whole L-shaped room = A + B.

Total = m²
