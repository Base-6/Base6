Part 1: Setting up the stalls (Area)

The ground is measured in square metres (m²).

Formula help:
• Parallelogram: base × height
• Trapezium: add the top and bottom, divide by 2, then multiply by the height.

1. The Hot Dog Stand is a parallelogram. Base = 6 m, height = 4 m.

6 × 4 = m²

2. The Ticket Booth is a trapezium. Top = 2 m, bottom = 4 m, height = 3 m.

  1. Add top and bottom: 2 + 4 = 6
  2. Divide by 2: 6 ÷ 2 = 3
  3. Multiply by height: 3 × 3 = ?
Area = m²
1. Hot Dog Stand: 6 × 4 = 24 m².   2. Ticket Booth: (2 + 4) ÷ 2 = 3, then 3 × 3 = 9 m².

Part 2: Storing the prizes (Volume)

Volume is how much space is inside a box, in cubic centimetres (cm³).

3. Cuboid box. Length = 10 cm, width = 5 cm, height = 4 cm.

10 × 5 × 4 = cm³

4. Triangular prism. Triangle base = 6 cm, triangle height = 4 cm, prism length = 10 cm.

Step 1 — triangle area: (6 × 4) ÷ 2 = cm²
Step 2 — area × length: 12 × 10 = cm³
3. Cuboid: 10 × 5 × 4 = 200 cm³.   4. Prism: (6 × 4) ÷ 2 = 12 cm², then 12 × 10 = 120 cm³.

Part 3: The 'Splat' game (Probability)

Players throw a wet sponge at a board that is 10 m by 10 m — a total area of 100 m². To win, they must hit the Golden Triangle (base 4 m, height 5 m).

5. Find the area of the Golden Triangle. (base × height ÷ 2)

(4 × 5) ÷ 2 = m²
