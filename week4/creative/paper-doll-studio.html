Welcome to your studio. Dress your character, choose your fabrics, and design an outfit that feels like yours. There is no wrong answer here — it is your design.

How it works: 1) Tap clothes in the Wardrobe to put them on. 2) Tap a fabric in the Fabric Drawer to load it onto your brush. 3) Tap a piece of clothing on your character to give it that fabric. Tap clothes again to take them off.
