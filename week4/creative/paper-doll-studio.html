Aesthetic and Creative · Week 4 · Design: Textiles, Fabrics & Costume Design

👗 Activity 1: Doll Designer

Use a template, choose garments, test materials, colours and patterns, then explain why each choice fits your design brief.

Learning objective

Fashion: I can use a template to create a simple fashion design, considering the materials, colours, or patterns to use.

Stage 1 of 5 · Design brief

Choose what your outfit needs to do

A designer does not only pick something that looks nice. They think about the person wearing it, the situation, the fabric, the colour, the pattern and how practical it will be.

🎯

Purpose

What is the outfit for? Party, performance, school, sport or weather?

🧵

Material

Should it be soft, strong, warm, shiny, waterproof or stretchy?

🎨

Colour

Colours can show mood, theme, identity, contrast or visibility.

✦

Pattern

Patterns add detail. They can be bold, calm, playful, smart or themed.

🚶

Practicality

Can the wearer move, keep warm, stay dry and clean it easily?

Teacher prompt: “Which factor matters most for this outfit, and how will we see that choice in the final design?”

Stage 2 of 5 · Template and clothes

Build the outfit on the template

Use the paper doll as your template. Add or remove clothes from the wardrobe. Think about what the person needs to wear before choosing fabrics.

Wardrobe

Tap to put clothes on or take them off. A dress replaces the T-shirt and trousers, just like a real outfit choice.

Stage 3 of 5 · Materials, colours and patterns

Load your fabric brush

Choose a colour, material or pattern, then tap a piece of clothing on the doll. Each choice should connect to at least one design factor: purpose, material, colour, pattern or practicality.

Connect each choice to a reason
