📝 Activity 2: Fashion Design Planner

Plan a fashion design, colour the template, add material and pattern choices, then review the learning objective.

Learning objective

Fashion: I can use a template to create a simple fashion design, considering the materials, colours, or patterns to use.

Stage 1 of 5 · Starter

Pick a design brief

Before drawing, choose what your outfit is for. This helps you make better choices about materials, colours and patterns.

Stage 2 of 5 · Planning table

Plan before you colour
