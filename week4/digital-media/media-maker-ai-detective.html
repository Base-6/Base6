Stage 2 of 5 · Audience

Make it work for Year 3

A good presenter changes words, images and amount of detail for the audience. Younger pupils usually need clearer pictures, shorter sentences and less clutter.

Stage 3 of 5 · AI vs real

Look closely at the images

AI-generated images can look convincing at first. Look for strange body parts, warped backgrounds, inconsistent lighting and odd textures.

11. Fluffy cat

Clue: the cat has five legs and the kitchen tiles are warped.

12. Ladybird on a leaf

Stage 4 of 5 · Checking routine

Four clues before you trust an image

Hands / bodies

Extra fingers, missing limbs or odd shapes.

Backgrounds

Warped lines, repeated patterns, impossible objects.

Lighting

Shadows that do not match the light source.

Textures / text

Blurry writing, unnatural skin, strange fur or plastic-looking objects.
