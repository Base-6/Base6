Stage 1 of 5 · Concept

How does the app know what to show next?

Streaming apps do not know your mind. They watch your behaviour. A like, skip, replay or long watch can become a data point. The algorithm uses those signals to guess what you might engage with next.

Stage 4 of 5 · Observation

What happens if everything is a dislike?

Now make the AI confused. Dislike every movie, then observe what the recommendation row does when it has no positive preference to follow.

Watch for this: when every genre is scored low, the system falls back to safer choices such as popularity, variety or trending content. It still wants to keep the user engaged.

Stage 5 of 5 · Debrief

Filter bubbles

A filter bubble happens when a system keeps showing you more of what it thinks you already like. That can be useful, but it can also narrow what you watch, buy or believe.
