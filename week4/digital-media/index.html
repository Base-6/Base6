📱 Digital Media

This week we are exploring

  • How recommendation algorithms build a profile from likes, dislikes and watch choices
  • Why simple chatbots follow rules rather than truly understanding meaning
  • How search engines crawl, rank and return websites
  • How to judge whether digital content, images and claims are reliable
  • How to spot clues that an image might be AI-generated
