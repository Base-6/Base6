Week 4 · Science

🧪 Science

This week we are exploring

  • Forces as pushes and pulls that change motion
  • Newton's First Law — inertia in space with no friction
  • Newton's Third Law — equal and opposite forces in collisions
  • Friction, gravity, air resistance, water resistance, upthrust and magnets
  • Contact and non-contact forces in real examples
