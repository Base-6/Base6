🏎️ Action-Reaction Bumper Cars

Crash two bumper cars, freeze the impact, and collect evidence for Newton's Third Law: for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

Stage 1 of 5 · Big idea

Every collision has a pair of forces

Newton's Third Law: when object A pushes object B, object B pushes back on object A with an equal force in the opposite direction.

Same size force

The force on the red car and the force on the blue car are equal at the moment of impact.

Different motion change

A smaller mass can change motion more, even though the force pair is the same size.

Stage 2 of 5 · Set up

Choose mass and speed

Set the masses and the red car's starting speed. The blue car begins stationary. Then write a quick prediction before you collide.
