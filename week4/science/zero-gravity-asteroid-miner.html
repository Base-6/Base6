Science · Week 4

🚀 The Zero-Gravity Asteroid Miner

Pilot a drifting ship in deep space. There is no gravity and no friction, so a force does not simply move the ship - it changes the ship's velocity.

Stage 1 of 5 · Mission briefing

Newton's First Law

In this mission the engines only fire for a short burst. After that, the ship keeps moving because there is no friction in space to slow it down.

Picture it: in deep space the dotted path does not fade away by itself. A force changes the ship's velocity vector.

🧊

Inertia

Objects keep doing what they are already doing unless a force changes them.

➡️

Velocity

Velocity means speed in a direction. A force can change speed, direction, or both.

🛑

No friction

The ship will not slow down by itself. To stop, use an equal force in the opposite direction.

Stage 2 of 5 · Thruster training

Try one burst at a time

Tap a thruster on the right panel or use your keyboard keys. The ship does not forget the force you gave it. Opposite thrusters subtract from velocity.

One burst adds to the motion. It does not switch itself off.

To slow down, you need a force in the opposite direction.

Stage 3 of 5 · The challenge

Dock at the Asteroid Mine

Navigate from the starting zone to the asteroid mine. Your ship must be inside the mine zone and completely stopped. Record exactly how many times you clicked each thruster.

Plan your route: add horizontal velocity, steer to line up, then apply equal opposite forces to brake perfectly inside the mine.

Mission rules

1. Use only the four thrusters.
2. Do not use Reset for your final attempt.
3. Stop inside the orange mine zone (Speed < 0.10).

Five right thrusts
