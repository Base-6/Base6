🏳️‍🌈 What is Pride?

A practical look at Pride, rights, language and school culture.

Choose a routeNo personal sharing neededRespectful questions
Before we start

You do not have to agree with everyone to treat them with respect.

This activity is not asking anyone to share personal beliefs or identity. It is about rights, language and how people are treated in school.

Understanding can take time. Respect is expected now.
