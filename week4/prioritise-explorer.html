Prioritise

What it means

To prioritise is to decide what matters most, and do that first.

We prioritise when we cannot do everything at once. Instead of trying to do it all, we choose the thing that matters most right now, and start there. There is no single right answer — what you prioritise can depend on the day, the task, and you.

Word family

priority · the thing that comes first priorities · more than one prioritising · doing it now prioritised · already done

In a sentence

  • On a busy morning, I prioritise getting dressed before I check my phone.
  • I prioritised my reading task because it was due first.
  • A good designer will prioritise the parts of a plan that matter most.

Where you might meet it this week

In your Project, when you choose which step to do first. In Preparation for Adulthood, when you plan your day or your free time. In Maths, when you work out which part of a problem to solve first. Noticing when you prioritise is a skill in itself.
