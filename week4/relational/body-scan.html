🌿 Mindful Body Scan

A few quiet minutes to thank your body for everything it does for you — not how it looks, but what it helps you do.

Before we begin

Getting comfy

Sit or rest however feels good. You can close your eyes, or keep them softly open looking at the calm circle. We'll move slowly through the body, one part at a time, noticing the helpful things each part does.

🌱 This is your time. There's no right way to do it. If any part feels uncomfortable, you can skip it or just breathe. You can stop whenever you like.
