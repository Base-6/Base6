Week 4 · Physical World & Exploration

🦋 Physical World & Exploration

Digital simulations for wildlife exploration and strength training, with optional real-world extensions at the end.

This week we are exploring

  • Creating a mini-conservation area and predicting which wildlife it may support
  • Recording and analysing changes in biodiversity using survey evidence
  • Choosing safe bodyweight strength movements and explaining technique
  • Designing a simple dumbbell or kettlebell circuit with control and balance
