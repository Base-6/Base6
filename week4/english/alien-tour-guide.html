👽 The Alien Tour Guide

An alien has landed and understands nothing. You are its guide. Change your purpose and watch how your writing has to change too.

Stage 1 of 5 · First Contact

The alien points at a bicycle

👽"Greetings, Earth-friend. What… is THIS round-legged metal beast?"

Three humans each describe the bicycle, but each has a different purpose. Read each one and tap the job it is doing.

"Careful! It has sharp pedals and it can fall over. Always wear a helmet!"
"A bicycle has two wheels, a seat and pedals. You push the pedals with your feet to move."
"Vroom! It is a two-wheeled tickle-machine that makes your hair go WHOOSH and your tummy go wheee!"

Stage 2 of 5 · Opposing Viewpoints

The alien and the human disagree

👽"This metal trap looks dangerous! I do not trust it."
🧒"A bike? It is a fun way to travel fast! I love mine."

Same bike, two viewpoints. Tap a word, then tap the zone where it belongs: just the alien, just the human, or both can agree.

Feeling and fact words

👽 Alien only

🤝 Both agree

🧒 Human only

Placed: 0 / 9

Stage 3 of 5 · Choose Your Path

The alien picks up a pencil

👽"A thin wooden stick. Tell me about it, guide. But first — what is your purpose?"

Pick a purpose. The alien will react differently, and your writing has to change to match. Try one path, then go back and try the other — see how different they sound.

👽

  • Match your purpose
  • Write at least two sentences

Stage 4 of 5 · One Object, Three Jobs

Now describe a spoon

👽"A shiny scooping tool! Describe it three ways so I truly understand."

Write one short line for each job. Notice how the same spoon sounds completely different each time.

Your guidebook entry

👽"Before I leave, write me a guidebook entry. Choose a thing, choose your purpose, and make every word match it."
  • Choose an object
  • Choose Inform or Warn
  • Use words that match your purpose
