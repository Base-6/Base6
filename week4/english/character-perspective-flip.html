🎭 Character Perspective Flip

The same story can feel very different depending on who is telling it. We will pick one character and tell the story through their eyes.

Stage 5 of 5 · Check your writing

Read it back and reflect

Read your story back to yourself. How did your character feel? Finish the sentence below.

Final check — tick what you did:

  • I used the word "I" to show the character is talking
  • I used a word from the word bank
  • I described a feeling
A good story is written from your chosen character's point of view, using "I" and "me", with details about what they see and how they feel.

For example: "My character felt sad because their bed was messy."
