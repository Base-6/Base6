🏰 Imagery to Ink: Creating Atmosphere

Your job is to write a description that gives the reader a sense of mystery. We will plan it step by step, then write it together.

Stage 1 of 5 · Look closely

Purpose and audience

Good writers think about why they are writing and who will read it. Tap a word to fill each gap.

My audience (the readers) are people who enjoy ___ stories.

My purpose (why I am writing) is to ___ the reader.

Tip: a spooky castle suits a reader who enjoys mystery, and a writer who wants to give the reader a little shiver.

Stage 3 of 5 · Figurative language

Similes and personification

📢 Simile: comparing things using "like" or "as" (for example, white as snow).
Personification: giving objects human actions (for example, the wind howled).
