Mission

This activity is based on the Writing for Audience and Purpose worksheet. It keeps the original questions, but makes the task interactive and easier to evidence.

Work through each section. The written answers are not auto-marked fully, but they are included in the evidence summary.

1. Purpose 2. Audience 3. Tone 4. Layout 5. Editing 6. Writing Finish

Section 1: Identifying Purpose

Quick reminder: Purpose means why a text was written: to instruct, persuade, entertain, inform or describe.
1. A recipe showing how to bake a chocolate cake.
2. A letter to the local council asking them to build a new skate park.
3. A spooky story about a haunted house.
4. A news report about a football match that happened yesterday.
5. A poster telling people about the dangers of smoking.

Section 2: Identifying Audience

Audience = the person or group who will read the text.
Word bank: Young children · School pupils · A business manager · Teenagers online
