Section 4: Comparing Interpretations

Read the two sources below about working conditions in factories during the Industrial Revolution (around 1840).

Source C: Written by a factory owner in 1841.

"The factories are clean, warm, and safe. The children who work here are healthy and happy to be earning good money to support their poor families. The machines are not difficult to use."

Source D: Written by a government inspector investigating factories in 1842.

"The machines are highly dangerous and have no safety guards. Children work for up to 14 hours a day in terrible heat. They are exhausted, and those who fall asleep are often beaten by the managers."
