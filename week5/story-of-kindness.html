Kindness Comic Maker

Create a short comic about a time someone was kind. It can be real, made up, serious, funny or dramatic. Build the story one panel at a time, then turn it into a written story if you want.

Short answers are fine. You can use speech bubbles, thought bubbles and narrator boxes instead of writing one long story straight away.
