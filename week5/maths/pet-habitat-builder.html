These are the three ideas you need for the mission. Very dramatic. Mostly rectangles.

📏

Area

Area means floor space. Count the grid squares, then check it with length × width.

💧

Capacity

Capacity means how much something can hold. Water bowls use ml / millilitres and L / litres.

🧠

Units matter

Each square is 1m² / one square metre. For water: 1000ml = 1L.
