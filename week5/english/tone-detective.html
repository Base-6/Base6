Mission

Decode tone without making it weird.

Inspect each phone message or email. Use the scanner dial, choose the clue, then judge if the message suits the audience.

Agent Jay briefing: Tone is how a message sounds. Look for clues in word choice, punctuation and layout.

Word choice

Words like please, thanks, whatever, unfortunately or amazing can change tone.

Punctuation

!!! can sound excited. Short full stops can sound blunt or annoyed.

Layout

Emails often need a greeting, clear information and a sign-off.
