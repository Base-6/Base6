How it works

Use the three steps to build a poem about your voice. You can type your own ideas, use the poem assistant, or mix both together. Your writing saves on this device as you go.

There is no single correct poem. Short is fine. Strange is fine. Honest is fine.

Step 1
Discover
Look around the room and out of the window. Notice a few things you can see, hear or touch.
Step 2
Develop
Pick one of your ideas and turn it into a picture of your voice. For example: my voice is like a kettle, because it gets louder when I am excited.
Optional support
Poem Assistant
Choose one style, then use any helper buttons that are useful. You can ignore anything you do not need.
Choose a poem style

Free verse guide

Try writing three short lines. One line can be a thing you noticed, one can be a feeling, and one can be what your voice wants to say.

Tiny challenge: add one word that shows how your voice feels.

Word bank

Type in Step 1 or Step 2 to create word chips here.

Click a word chip to add it to your poem.

Sound finder

Type a word to find rhymes or near-rhymes. Near-rhymes are allowed.

Try a word like light, voice, day, rain, room, loud or see.

Step 3
Write
Write your poem here. You can use the assistant, write from scratch, or edit the starter lines.
