How it works

Three small steps to a poem about your voice. Fill in as much or as little as you like. Your writing saves on this device as you go, and you can send it to Jay at the end.

Step 1
Discover
Look around the room and out of the window. Notice a few things you can see, hear or touch.
Step 2
Develop
Pick one of those things and turn it into a picture of your voice. For example, my voice is like a kettle, because it gets louder when I am excited.
Step 3
Write
Turn your idea into a few lines of a poem. It can be as short as you like.
Your writing is saved on this device.
