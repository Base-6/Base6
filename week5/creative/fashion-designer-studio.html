Fashion design this week

Create an outfit idea using a template. There is no single correct design. Choose a style, experiment with colour and pattern, draw in your own details, then explain why your choices work. Take it in any order you like.

Learning objective

Fashion: I can use a template to create a simple fashion design, considering the materials, colours, or patterns to use.

Creative choices

Choose a vibe, invent a title, add accessories, draw your own details, create a logo, and explain what makes the design work.

Step 1 of 5 · Brief

Who is this design for?

Start with the person, event or problem. This gives your design a reason to exist.

Step 2 of 5 · Moodboard

Choose the creative direction

Pick a vibe, inspiration and texture. This gives you more creative control before you start colouring.

Designer toolkit

Use these four ideas to make choices that actually fit the brief.

MaterialWhat the outfit is made from, like cotton, denim, waterproof fabric or recycled fabric.
ColourThe visual mood. Bright can feel energetic. Dark can feel dramatic. Soft can feel calm.
PatternRepeated lines, shapes or images, like stripes, checks, stars, flowers or symbols.
PracticalityHow the design helps the wearer, like movement, warmth, pockets, stretch or protection.

1 · Pick a vibe
