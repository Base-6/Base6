What is plastic?

Hello, I am Captain Inky. Let us find out what plastic really is. Three quick games, then a think.

Tip for grown ups: this works on a big screen for the class, or on a tablet for one person. You can read the words out loud.

Game 1: Sort it

Tap a thing. Then tap the bin it belongs in. Plastic, or not plastic?

Not plastic

Game 2: What is it made of?

Tap each card to turn it over and see. The plastic ones go pink.

Game 3: Where does plastic come from?

Tap each step to follow the journey.

The big idea

Tap both boxes. Plastic is not all bad and not all good.

Plastic helps us

Tap to see how
  • It keeps food fresh for longer.
  • It is light, so it is easy to carry.
  • It does not smash when you drop it.
  • Hospitals use it to keep people safe.

Plastic is a problem

Tap to see why
  • It lasts a very, very long time.
  • It breaks into tiny bits that never go away.
  • It can hurt sea animals.

Both boxes are true at once. Plastic is useful AND a problem. That is what makes it tricky.

Your turn to think

What do you think about plastic now? You can write, or draw, or both.
