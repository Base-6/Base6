The big idea

Open both boxes. Plastic isn't all bad, and it isn't all good either.

Plastic helps us

Tap to see how
  • It keeps food fresh for longer.
  • It is light, so it is easy to carry.
  • It does not smash when you drop it.
  • Hospitals use it to keep people safe.

Plastic is a problem

Tap to see why
  • It lasts a very, very long time.
  • It breaks into tiny bits that never go away.
  • It can hurt sea animals.

Both boxes are true at once. Plastic is useful AND a problem. That is what makes it tricky.

The science bit. Plastic is a polymer: long chains of molecules, made mostly from crude oil, which is basically ancient squashed dead stuff pumped out of the ground. It was built to be cheap and to last. It nailed it. That is also the problem.

Rank them. Tap these in the order you reckon they last out in the world, from gone quickest to lasts longest. Then reveal what people usually claim.

Now question the famous number. You will see "a plastic bottle takes 450 years to break down" all over the internet. Plot twist: nobody actually measured that. The figure was never backed by solid science, and the people linked to it have quietly walked away from it. Lesson: be suspicious of a number that looks a bit too tidy.

Final challenge. Make the case FOR plastic in three lines, then against it in three lines. If you still cannot pick a side, congratulations, that is the honest answer.
