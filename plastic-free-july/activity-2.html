Reusable only counts if you reuse it. A cotton tote bag has to be used about 131 times before it beats a single-use plastic bag for its effect on the climate (that is from a UK government study). So the planet points come from the 131st trip to the shop, not from owning fourteen tote bags in fourteen colours. We see you.

Spot the marketing. Loads of "eco" products are just normal products with a green leaf printed on them. "Recyclable" does not mean it will be recycled. "Plant based" can still be plastic. Read the small print, not the big leaf.

Your challenge. Two jobs. One: name the single swap you would genuinely keep doing, not the one that looks good in a photo. Two: find one "eco" thing that is mostly marketing. Bonus point if you can explain what is wrong with paper straws.
