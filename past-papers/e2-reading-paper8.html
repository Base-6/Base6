How to save money on food shopping

This guide will help you to spend less when you buy food.

The first step is to plan your meals. After this, you should make a list of what you need. This will help you:

  • save money by only buying food you need
  • waste less food
  • save time if you are busy.

It is an excellent idea to check online for special deals before going shopping. You can also use online stores to save on travel costs.

Don't do all your shopping in supermarkets or in shops. You can often save money by buying fruit and vegetables in markets.
