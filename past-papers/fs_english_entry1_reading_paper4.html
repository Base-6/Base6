Text B

Rafton Fun Park: How to get here

By car
The park is on Green Road. It is only four miles from Rafton.
By bus
Catch the Number 2 or Number 3 bus from Rafton. You can also catch the Number 17 bus from Cleyton.
By train
The nearest train station is Hanton. It is five miles away. You can get a taxi from there.
By bike
There is a bike path near to the park.

Go to the website to get a free map: www.raftonfunpark.biz
