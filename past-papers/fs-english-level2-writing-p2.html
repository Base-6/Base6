Task 1 21 marks ✓

You volunteer at your local community centre.

The community centre needs repairs and new equipment. There is also limited access for people with disabilities.

You have been asked to contact a local business group to ask for financial help and other support.

Write an email to the local business group to persuade them to help.

In your email, you should:

  • describe the problems
  • explain how the business group can help
  • say how businesses will benefit.
Aim to write about 250 to 300 words.
