Think about recording, performing, podcasting, music, gaming, streaming or presentations.

🔎 Research — What Does a Microphone Stand Need to Do?

⚖️

Stay balanced

The base needs enough weight and width so the stand does not fall over when the microphone is attached.

📏

Hold the right height

The microphone should sit at a useful height for speaking, singing or recording at a table.

🧲

Grip the microphone

The holder needs to stop the microphone from sliding, rolling or pointing the wrong way.

🛡️

Be safe to use

Edges should be smooth, joins should be secure, and heavy parts should not wobble or drop.

Look at examples: desk stands, floor stands, boom arms, tripod stands. What shapes and parts do they use?
