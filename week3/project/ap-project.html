🏛️ What is Somerset Youth Parliament?

Somerset Youth Parliament (SYP) is run by Somerset Council. It has two parts:

Members of Youth Parliament (MYPs) — 3 elected reps, one for each area of Somerset. They represent Somerset at the national UK Youth Parliament, including an annual debate in the House of Commons. The most recent MYPs (Blake, Eva and Luca) were elected in late 2025 and started in April 2026.

The Advisory Group — open all year to anyone aged 10–25 who lives, works or studies in Somerset. No election needed. AP can join this right now.

Yeovil is in the South Somerset and Mendip area — that's AP's constituency for the MYP election.

🗳️ How the MYP election works

It is a self-nomination election — any young person aged 11–17 in Somerset can put themselves forward. A teacher does not have to do it for you.

Nominations open every September (in 2025 the deadline was 3 November). Voting then runs through schools, colleges and youth clubs across Somerset during November–December. In the 2025 election, over 1,600 young people voted across 11 schools.

All candidates are invited to a candidates' development day — a full Saturday of workshops on campaigning and public speaking. Even if you don't win, you get a certificate and an invitation to join the Advisory Group.

The next election is expected around November–December 2027. AP would be 15–16 and comfortably within the eligible age range.

📅 What the role actually involves

Monthly meetings (online or face-to-face, sometimes at County Hall in Taunton). Consultations, grant decisions, recruitment panels, public speaking, campaign design. MYPs also attend national UK Youth Parliament events — including sitting in the House of Commons for the annual debate.

The Advisory Group has the same meetings but without the election commitment — it's a good way to build up experience before standing.

The role is voluntary and unpaid. Travel to Taunton (about 40 minutes from Yeovil) is sometimes needed for in-person events.
