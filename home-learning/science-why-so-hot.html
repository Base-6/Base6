Step 7 · definition

What is a heatwave, apart from rude?

One hot day is normal. A heatwave is several hot days in a row, because apparently one day was not enough.

Key idea: heatwaves are about heat lasting, not just one dramatic afternoon.

High pressure sits over us like a lid. The sky stays clear, the air sinks, and the heat builds up. Basically: saucepan, but make it weather.

Step 10 · big picture

A warming world, because plot twist

Hot days like today are happening more often than they used to. The graph is not being subtle.

In July 2022, England passed 40°C for the first time on record, including in Lincolnshire. The weather really said: new high score.

The planet is slowly warming, like an extra blanket holding in heat. Useful on a sofa. Less useful for the entire Earth.

The good news: our choices can help slow this down. The planet appreciates not being roasted.
