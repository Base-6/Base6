Right then. Pride Week.

Good news first: this is not an assembly. Nobody is going to make you stand in a circle and share your feelings.

This is about one simple idea. Being the change. What that actually means, and the surprisingly small things that make a school a kinder place to be.

It takes about half an hour. You can go at your own speed and tap Back if you miss something. Onward.

So what is Pride actually about?

Pride has excellent flags. We will get to the flags. But it started as something bigger than a parade.

Celebration. Being proud of who you are, no apology needed.

Standing up. Pride began as a protest for being treated fairly and equally.

Visibility. Showing that everyone belongs, exactly as they are.

So yes, also the flags. We said we would get to them.

What being the change is not

It is not changing your profile picture for one week and then forgetting about it until next June.

Being the change is the small, everyday stuff. The quiet choices nobody hands out a certificate for. And honestly? Those count the most.

Plot twist: a big chunk of it is just being decent to people. Wild, we know.
