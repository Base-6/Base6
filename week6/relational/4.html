The Future Letter

A letter to the you of the future, to be opened on the last day of the school year.

You are going to write a short letter to yourself. Not to hand in, not to be marked. Jay will keep a copy safe and give it back to you on the last day of the school year, so future you can see how far you have come. Be honest and be kind to yourself. Future you will appreciate it.

Write to the future you. Imagine yourself reading this on the last day of the year. What would you want that person to know, to remember, or to smile about? Fill in as much or as little as feels right.
Dear future me,
Right now, this is what life feels like
This is something I am hoping for
And this is what I want you to remember
With hope, past me.
📜

When you send this, it saves here on this device and a sealed copy goes to Jay to look after. You will get it back at the end of the year. No one will use it against you, it is just for future you.
