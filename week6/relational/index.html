Week 6 · Relational Check-In

Setting Goals

A short check-in each morning this week, building from a dream all the way to a first step.

This week's theme is Setting Goals. Each day builds on the one before, from picturing where you want to be, to planning backwards, to committing to a single first step. You can do them in order, or dip into whichever fits the day. Each takes about twenty minutes, and there is never any pressure to share.

This week's check-ins

1 🗺️ Motivation Monday The Milestone Map Brainstorm where you would like to be, in learning, friendships and yourself, by this time next year. Today Open check-in → 2 🧭 Taskmaster Tuesday The Back-Plan Board Start at a future goal and work backwards to the exact small step you could take this month. Today Open check-in → 3 💭 Wondering Wednesday Dream or Goal? What is the difference between a dream and a goal, and when does a wish become a plan? A talking day. Today Open check-in → 4 📜 Therapeutic Thursday The Future Letter Write a letter to yourself, to be kept safe and opened on the last day of the school year. Today Open check-in → 5 🚀 Focus Friday The Launchpad Choose the single most important action you will take on Monday morning to start your new goal. Today Open check-in →
