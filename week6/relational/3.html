Dream or Goal?

What turns a wish into a plan? A talking-and-thinking check-in, with barely any typing.

Today is more about thinking and talking than writing. A dream and a goal can start out looking the same. The interesting question is where they split apart. Have a go at the sorter, then chew on the discussion cards, out loud or in your head.

One way to see it. A dream is something you would love to happen. A goal is a dream you have decided to do something about. The difference is often a plan and a first step, which is what the rest of this week is about.

Quick sorter: dream or goal?

Discussion cards

No right answers here. Talk them through with someone, or just think them over.

Is it when you say it out loud? Write it down? Tell someone? Take the first step?
Can a dream be too big to ever become a goal? Or can any dream be broken down?

Is it worth having dreams you may never reach?
