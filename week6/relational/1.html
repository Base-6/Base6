The Milestone Map

Where would you like to be by this time next year? Let us sketch it out.

This is a brainstorm, not a test. There are no wrong answers, and nothing here is fixed. You are just imagining where you might like to be a year from now, in three parts of life. Jot down whatever comes to mind. A few words per box is plenty.

A year is a long time. Think how much has changed since last summer. The point of a map is not to predict the future perfectly, it is to give yourself a direction to head in.
📚Learning

Something you would like to get better at, understand, or achieve with your learning.

👥People and friendships

Something about how you connect with others: friends, family, or your group.

🌱You, personally

Something just for you: a skill, a habit, a hobby, or a way you would like to feel.

Three areas to map. Fill whichever speak to you.

Keep your map

Your map is saved on this device so you can come back to it. You could also send a copy to Jay, so it is somewhere safe for later in the year.
