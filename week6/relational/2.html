Taskmaster Tuesday · Setting Goals

The Back-Plan Board

Start at the finish line, then work backwards to what you could do this month.

Big goals feel huge because we look at the whole mountain at once. Back-planning flips it around. You start at the goal, then step backwards, asking each time: what would have to happen just before this? By the end you reach something small enough to start this month.

Work top to bottom. Write your goal at the top, then fill the rungs going down. Each rung is a step earlier in time, until the bottom one is something you could genuinely begin in the next few weeks.
