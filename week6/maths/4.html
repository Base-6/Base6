Set up the chance lab

Probability is a way of describing how likely something is to happen. You can use words, fractions, decimals or a scale from 0 to 1.

Today’s big idea: two outcomes does not always mean a 50/50 chance. The outcomes need to be equally likely.

Round 3: Fair and unfair spinners
