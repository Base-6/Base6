Word of the Week · Initiate

Ocean Defence

Plastic is drifting into the bay. Place your sea-creature crew to clean it up before it reaches the open water.

The word is Initiate: to make the first move

Nothing here happens until you start it. You choose where the first tower goes, then set the first wave moving. The whole defence begins with one decision from you. That is what it means to initiate: everything that follows grows from the first push.

Clear a tier to unlock a leaf. Start with Normal to learn the ropes, then test yourself. Almost Impossible is meant to be beaten only after plenty of practice.

How to play. Tap a stone pad beside the path to place a tower. Tap a tower you own to upgrade or sell it. Start the wave when you are ready, and speed it up once you feel confident.
